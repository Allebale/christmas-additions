## Christmas Additions 1.8.1

Mod API: 1.8

- Built with the final mod SDK 1.8.0, so the game no longer asks for a rebuild. Nothing else changed.

## Christmas Additions 1.8.0

**New enemies (for levels that place them)**
- **Gingerbread men** walk about where a level puts invisible coins named "Gingerbread N". They spot Mario, run after him and swing at him (a knock back, then a rest so you can get up). Jump on one and he falls apart; spray him for 5 seconds and he melts; water pushes him back, and into a wall he goes splat. They leave puddles, make sounds and drop a coin, and come back after a while.
- **Bauble Bob-ombs** waddle about where a level puts invisible coins named "Bauble N", wind-up key turning. Near Mario they light their fuse, chase him and explode. Water puts the fuse out and pushes them along; into a wall, or jumped on, they shatter like glass and drop a coin.
- Only the nearest of them are drawn, so the snowmen keep their faces within the game's model limits.

**FLUDD Weapons' black goop is ice** (with FLUDD Weapons 1.4.0): white-blue ice that freezes enemies and makes the floor slippery.

**New settings**
- Gingerbread men walk about, and chase and hit Mario (On)
- Bauble Bob-ombs walk about, and light up, chase Mario and explode (On)
- FLUDD Weapons' black goop is ice (On)
- Beaten gingerbread men and bauble Bob-ombs stay gone till the level loads (Off)

Built with the game's API 1.8 beta 3.
